# Ritch: guide

Ritch is a small offline app on your phone that tells you what to do today, tracks what you actually did, and shows over about 12 weeks whether it's working. It lives at https://teddymilford.github.io/ritch/ and runs from the home screen like a native app. Everything you log stays on the phone.

## The goals

The target date is **Thursday, December 31, 2026**. The goals are:

| Goal | Kind |
|---|---|
| 5K in under 35 minutes | Running |
| 25 strict pull-ups | Strength |
| 50 parallel-bar dips | Strength |
| 100 push-ups | Strength |

These are aspirational, and the plan is honest about that. Couch to 5K gets you to 30 minutes of continuous running by mid-December. That isn't the same as a 5K: sub-35 needs a pace faster than 7:00 per km (11:16 per mile). It's possible by Dec 31 but not assured.

The rep goals are long-term targets. The plan doesn't force a curve to hit them by a date, because where you end up depends on where you start. What the app guarantees is a steady, safe progression and a clear record. On Dec 31 you'll know exactly how far you came, even if the numbers aren't 25/50/100 yet.

## The calendar

Weeks run **Sunday through Saturday**.

| Day | What |
|---|---|
| Sunday | Strength C: push-up volume day |
| Monday | Run, then push-up practice |
| Tuesday | Strength A |
| Wednesday | Run, then push-up practice |
| Thursday | Strength B: longer sets |
| Friday | Rest |
| Saturday | Run, then push-up practice |

There's always a non-running day between runs. The one exception to Sunday lifting is Sunday, Oct 18, which stays a rest day so the first workout is still Monday, Oct 19. Sunday lifting starts Oct 25.

| Dates | Week | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| Oct 11-17 | Practice | Optional and easier than the plan. Learn the movements and the app. |
| Oct 18-24 | 1 | First workout Monday, Oct 19. Couch to 5K week 1; first lifting week has one fewer set. |
| Nov 15-21 | 5 | Optional rep check-in for lifting |
| Nov 22-28 | 6 | Lighter lifting week (Thanksgiving week) |
| Dec 13-19 | 9 | Couch to 5K week 9: 30 minutes continuous |
| Dec 20-26 | 10 | Consolidation (the app's proposal, not NHS) |
| Dec 27-Jan 2 | 11 | Second rep check-in; optional 5K check on Wednesday, Dec 30 |
| After | 12+ | The plan keeps going with a repeating maintenance week |

Repeating a week pushes these dates back by a week. Nothing gets squeezed to fit Dec 31.

## How the plan works

**Running.** Weeks 1-9 follow the NHS Couch to 5K plan exactly: run/walk intervals that grow until you run 30 minutes straight. Every run has a 5-minute brisk walk before and after. Effort is easy and conversational, and walking breaks are part of the plan.

**Strength.** Pull-ups, dips and push-ups three times a week, plus a little leg and core work. Your pull-up bar, dip bars and parallettes are all used (L-sits on Sunday).

| Session | Pull-ups | Dips | Push-ups | Extras |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Sun, Strength C | 4 sets | 3 sets | 5 sets, done first | L-sit, dead hang |
| Tue, Strength A | 5 sets | 4 sets | 3 sets | Goblet squat, Romanian deadlift, hollow hold |
| Thu, Strength B | 1 longer set + 3 | 3 sets | 1 longer set + 3 | Split squat, hip bridge, side plank |
| After each run | | | 2 easy sets at half your target | |

Week 1, the lighter Thanksgiving week and the check-in weeks use fewer sets.
- **Working sets.** Sets start at about 60% of your clean max and always leave 2-3 reps in reserve. You never train to failure.
- **Progression.** If every session in a week is clean, next week goes up by one step: +1 rep per set for pull-ups and dips, +2 for push-ups. If every one is short, it steps down. Anything else holds. Hard sessions, pain or skips never push the numbers up.
- **"I don't know yet" is fine.** Unknown maxes give effort-based sets ("stop with 2-3 clean reps left"), and your first logged reps set the targets. Blank never means zero.
- **Can't do the full movement yet?** If your max is under 3 pull-ups, 3 dips or 5 push-ups, you train an easier version (negatives, band-assisted, incline) until a check-in shows you're ready.

**Rep standards** (in About) define a clean rep. Logging the same kind of rep every time is what makes the numbers mean something.

**Stop** for joint pain, sharp pain, pain that gets worse, or anything that changes how you move. Normal soreness is expected. The app can't diagnose anything.

## Using it day to day

Open the app and it shows **today**. It works out today in Chicago time and handles midnight and daylight saving changes. Future days are view-only.

The app works like a native one: the page itself never scrolls. Each screen is sized to fit an iPhone; only long lists (History, the sources in About) scroll inside their area. A tab bar along the bottom switches between **Today, Progress, History, Setup and About**. At the top, the week strip shows Sunday to Saturday; tap a day, or use the ‹ › at either end to step a day at a time.

Each workout is a short **deck of cards**, one screen each. Swipe left and right, or use the ‹ › arrows above the tab bar. The dots show where you are and fill in as each card's sets are done.

- **Run day:** Overview, Session (timer and intervals), Push-ups practice, Finish.
- **Lifting day:** Overview (the whole session at a glance), Warm-up, one card per exercise, Finish.

### On a run day

1. Swipe to **Session** and tap **Start session timer** when you start the warm-up. It keeps counting with the screen locked or the app closed, and the app reopens on this card while it runs.
2. Follow the intervals and tick off the warm-up, main set and cool-down.
3. Tap **Pause** at the end. This fills in Time on the Finish card.
4. On **Finish**, add distance if you like, mark how it felt (Easy, About right, Hard), and tick the pain box if something hurt in a way that changed how you moved.
5. Tap **Finish workout**. The app returns to the overview with the result.

### On a lifting day

1. Read the overview, then swipe to the **Warm-up** card and do it.
2. Swipe to each exercise. For pull-ups, dips and push-ups, tap **Count reps · set 1 of 3** to open the counter:
   - **Tap the big box once per rep**, or tap the count in right after the set. You can't tap while hanging from a bar. For push-ups, put the phone under your chest and touch it at the bottom of each rep.
   - **−1** fixes a miscount.
   - **Save set** records the set and starts the **rest timer**. The timer turns green when the planned rest is up.
   - After the last set, mark how it felt, then go **Back to workout**, which returns to that exercise's card.
3. For the other exercises, tap the set buttons as you finish each set. Open "Log reps or weight" if you want to record details.
4. On the **Finish** card, tap **Finish workout**. The app records how long the session took.

Each set button shows the reps you actually did, so a finished pull-up line reads like `6 6 5`.

### When a day goes sideways

All of these are on the **Finish** card.

- **Mark skipped.** Nothing moves or doubles up. The next session is unchanged.
- **Edit** today or a future workout to change its exercises or turn it into a rest day. It changes only that date.
- **Repeat this week…** If a running week felt too hard, repeat it. Saturday after the run, the overview also recaps the week and asks *Continue* or *Repeat*. On Sunday, the first card recaps last week and you can still repeat it. Repeating is normal, not failure.

## Tracking progress

**Progress** is the 12-week answer, split into tabs that each fit one screen:

- **Summary.** Workouts done so far, your current targets, and total reps and runs against the goals.
- **Days.** The consistency grid: each week is a row and each day a cell, marked done, skipped or not logged. Tap a cell to open that day.
- **Lifts.** Pick pull-ups, dips or push-ups to see your setup number, current clean max, best set and total reps next to the goal, with a chart of your best set per session. Filled dots are rep checks.
- **Runs.** Runs done, total logged time, longest session, and every run with time, distance and how it felt.
- **Weeks.** Workouts done, reps per movement and run time for each week.

The fairest before-and-after comparison is the **optional rep checks** in weeks 5 and 11, done the same way each time. **History** lists every logged day with notes.

## The practice week (Oct 11-17)

The practice week is easier than week 1:

| Day | Session |
|---|---|
| Mon, Wed, Sat | 6 × 30-second easy jogs with walks, about 22 minutes |
| Sunday, Tuesday | Easy sets: stop with 3-4 clean reps left |
| Thursday | Optional rep check: one set each to the last clean rep |

Use it to learn the standards, the counter and the timer. After Saturday's run, the app summarizes the week. If you did the rep check, it offers **Use as my starting numbers**, which sets your week 1 targets. Otherwise, the practice week never affects the real plan.

## Setup (optional)

**Setup** has five tabs: **Reps** (current clean reps for each movement), **Running** (how long you can jog, and which Couch to 5K week to start at), **Gear** (pull-up bar, dip bars, bands, weights), **Notes** (aches or limitations) and **Backup**. Switching tabs keeps what you've typed; one Save covers all four.

Every field can stay blank ("I don't know yet"). Missing equipment swaps in substitutes. Your limitations note is shown on each workout's overview as a reminder.

## Your data

- Everything is saved **only on this phone**. There's no account, no sync and no analytics.
- The home-screen app has its own storage, separate from the Safari tab. Log in one or the other.
- **Setup → Backup → Export JSON** saves a backup through the share sheet. **Import JSON** restores it. Export now and then, and before changing phones.

## Testing mode

Add `?test` to the URL to rehearse without touching real data. For example, https://teddymilford.github.io/ritch/?test=2026-10-20 opens on the first lifting day. A dashed bar at the top lets you step the simulated day back or forward with ‹ ›, **Reset** clears the practice data, and **Exit** returns to the real app. Testing data is stored separately and never mixes with your real log.

## Install on iPhone

1. Open https://teddymilford.github.io/ritch/ in **Safari**.
2. Tap Share, then **Add to Home Screen**, then Add. The icon is the red, white and blue 33.
3. Open it once from the home screen while online. After that it works offline. Updates arrive the next time it's opened online.

## Changing the plan

The plan is plain JSON in `src/plan/`:

| File | Contents |
|---|---|
| `running.json` | Running weeks |
| `strength.json` | Rep standards, sessions and the progression rule |
| `practice.json` | The practice week |
| `about.json` | Dates, schedule, safety text and sources |

Edit a file, run `npm test` and `npm run verify`, then push to `main` and the site redeploys. The **About** screen keeps research-backed guidance (NHS, ACSM, HHS, BJSM) separate from the app's own programming choices.
